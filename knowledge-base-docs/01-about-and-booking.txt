Palmway Transfers: About Us and How to Book

Palmway Transfers is a fictional airport transfer company used in this tutorial. We provide private rides between Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) and hotels, homes and towns across Sri Lanka, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How to book
1. Enter your pickup point and destination. For airport pickups, choose "Arrival at CMB".
2. Choose a vehicle that fits your group and luggage (Mini car, Sedan or Van).
3. Add your flight number, arrival date and time, number of passengers, number of suitcases, and any extras such as child seats.
4. Choose a payment method and confirm.

You receive a booking reference (for example PW-123456) and a confirmation email within a few minutes. Your driver's name and vehicle number are sent 12 hours before pickup.

When to book
Book at least 6 hours before pickup. Bookings made less than 6 hours ahead are accepted only if a driver is free; we confirm by email within 30 minutes.

Return trips
You can add a return trip (for example hotel to airport) in the same booking. A return trip is priced as a separate one-way ride.

Changing a booking
You can change the pickup time, flight number, passenger count or extras for free up to 6 hours before pickup by replying to your confirmation email or contacting support. A change of vehicle type may change the price.

Palmway Transfers: Child Seats

Child seats are free, but you must request them when you book so the driver can bring and fit them.

Seat types
- Infant seat: babies up to about 13 kg (around 12 months).
- Child seat: children from about 9 to 18 kg (around 1 to 4 years).
- Booster seat: children from about 15 to 36 kg (around 4 to 12 years).

Tell us the age and approximate weight of each child in the booking form. We can provide up to 2 child seats in a Mini car or Sedan and up to 4 in a Van.

Fitting
The driver fits the seat before pickup. Parents should check the seat and buckle their child in. You may also bring your own seat.

Last-minute requests
If you forget to add a child seat, contact support at least 6 hours before pickup. We cannot guarantee seats requested later.

Safety
For safety, children must not sit in the front seat, and every passenger must wear a seatbelt or be in a proper child seat.

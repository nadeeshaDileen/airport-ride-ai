Palmway Transfers: Waiting Time Policy

Airport pickups
The driver waits up to 60 minutes after your flight's actual landing time at no extra cost. This covers immigration, baggage collection and customs. Because the driver tracks your flight, a delayed flight does not use up your free waiting time. The 60 minutes start when the plane actually lands.

After the free 60 minutes, waiting costs LKR 500 for each started 30 minutes, up to a maximum of 2 extra hours. If you have not contacted us by then, the driver may leave and the booking is treated as a no-show.

If immigration or baggage is slow, message your driver or support. If you tell us, the driver will keep waiting, and the extra waiting fee is charged only for time over the free 60 minutes.

Hotel and home pickups
For pickups that are not at the airport, the driver waits 15 minutes after the booked pickup time at no extra cost. After that, waiting costs LKR 500 for each started 30 minutes.

Flight cancelled
If your flight is cancelled, contact support. We move your booking to your new flight at no charge, or cancel it with a full refund.

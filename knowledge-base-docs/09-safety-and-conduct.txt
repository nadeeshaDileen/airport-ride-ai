Palmway Transfers: Safety and Conduct

Our drivers
All drivers hold a valid professional driving licence, have passed a background check, and are trained in safe driving. Vehicles are inspected every 3 months and are fully insured.

During the ride
- Every passenger must wear a seatbelt.
- Drivers follow speed limits and do not use a phone while driving.
- Smoking and alcohol are not allowed in the vehicle.
- Drivers take a rest break on rides longer than 3 hours.

Sharing your trip
Your driver's name, photo and vehicle number are sent before pickup. Check them before you get in.

Respect for drivers and passengers
We expect respectful behaviour from everyone. Abuse, threats or harassment of drivers or passengers are not tolerated. A driver may end a ride if they feel unsafe, and the passenger may be refused future bookings.

Reporting a problem
If anything during your ride makes you feel unsafe, ask the driver to stop in a safe public place and contact support. In an emergency, call the local emergency number first. You can also report a problem after the ride by replying to your receipt email.

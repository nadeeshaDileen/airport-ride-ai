Palmway Transfers: Cancellations and Refunds

Cancelling a booking
- More than 24 hours before pickup: free cancellation, full refund.
- Between 24 hours and 6 hours before pickup: 50% of the fare is refunded.
- Less than 6 hours before pickup: no refund.

To cancel, reply to your confirmation email with the word CANCEL and your booking reference, or contact support.

No-show
If you do not arrive and do not contact us before the waiting time ends, the booking is a no-show and the full fare is charged. Cash bookings that are a no-show may need prepayment for future bookings.

When we cancel
If we cannot provide a driver, we tell you as early as possible and give a full refund. We also try to find you another licensed transfer at no extra cost.

Cancelled or diverted flights
If your flight is cancelled or diverted, there is no cancellation fee. Contact support to move or cancel the booking.

How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 10 working days to appear, depending on your bank. Cash payments are refunded by bank transfer.

Palmway Transfers: Vehicles and Pricing

Vehicle types
- Mini car: up to 3 passengers and 2 medium suitcases. Best for solo travellers and couples with light luggage.
- Sedan: up to 3 passengers and 3 large suitcases. Air-conditioned, more legroom.
- Van: up to 8 passengers and 8 large suitcases. Best for families and groups, or anyone with surfboards or golf bags.

If the number of passengers or suitcases is more than the vehicle allows, the booking form asks you to choose a larger vehicle. For safety, the driver cannot take more people than there are seats with seatbelts.

How prices work
Each ride has a base fare plus a rate per kilometre for the chosen vehicle. Popular routes (for example the airport to Colombo city, Negombo, Kandy or Galle) have a fixed price shown before you book. The price you see at booking is the price you pay. It includes fuel, highway tolls, airport parking and the driver's time.

Night surcharge
Pickups between 22:00 and 05:59 have a 10% night surcharge. It is included in the price shown at booking.

Extra stops
One short stop (up to 15 minutes, for example to buy a SIM card or withdraw cash) is free. Each additional stop costs a flat fee shown in the booking form.

Currency
Prices are shown in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). Card payments can be made in LKR or USD at the exchange rate shown at checkout.

Palmway Transfers: Airport Pickup Procedure

Where to meet your driver
After you collect your luggage and pass through customs at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), walk into the public arrivals hall. Your driver waits at the meeting point near the arrivals exit, holding a Palmway Transfers sign with your name on it.

What the driver does before you land
Your driver tracks your flight using the flight number in your booking. If your flight lands early or late, the driver adjusts the pickup time. You do not need to call us about flight delays.

If you cannot find your driver
1. Check that you are in the public arrivals hall, not inside the baggage area.
2. Look for the Palmway Transfers sign near the arrivals exit.
3. Call or message the driver using the number sent 12 hours before pickup.
4. If you still cannot reach the driver, contact Palmway support (see the Contact document).

Please do not leave with another driver or taxi without telling us, or the booking may be marked as a no-show.

Walking to the car
The driver helps with your luggage and walks with you to the car park, which takes about 5 minutes. Airport parking fees are included in your price.

If your flight number changes
If your airline changes your flight, send the new flight number to support as soon as possible so the driver tracks the right flight.

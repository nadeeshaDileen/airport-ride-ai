Palmway Transfers: Luggage Policy

How much luggage fits
- Mini car: 2 medium suitcases plus small hand luggage.
- Sedan: 3 large suitcases plus small hand luggage.
- Van: 8 large suitcases, or fewer suitcases plus large items.

A large suitcase is a checked bag up to about 30 kg and 80 cm tall. Small hand luggage (backpacks, laptop bags, handbags) does not count towards the limit.

Large or special items
Surfboards, bicycles, golf bags, prams and wheelchairs must be listed in the "special requests" field when booking. They usually need a Van. Folding wheelchairs and prams are carried for free.

Too much luggage on the day
If you bring more luggage than the vehicle can safely carry, the driver may not be able to take all of it. We will try to send a larger vehicle, and you pay the price difference. Please count your bags before you book.

Lost items
If you leave something in the car, contact support with your booking reference. We keep found items for 30 days.
